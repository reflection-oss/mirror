# Code of Conduct

This policy applies to Mirror's GitHub issues and other project discussions.
Everyone participating, including maintainers, is expected to follow it.

Mirror welcomes bug reports and feature requests. For now, we are not accepting
external code or documentation contributions or pull requests. See
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for how to report issues.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people respectfully, regardless of their background or experience.
- Keep feedback constructive and discussions relevant to Mirror.
- Discuss ideas and behavior without attacking people.
- Respect others' privacy. Do not share personal information without consent.
- Take responsibility for what you post, including AI-assisted content.
- Respect others' time. Avoid repeated pings or demands for immediate replies.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal insults.
- Sexual harassment or unwelcome sexual content.
- Sharing another person's private information without permission.
- Spam, trolling, or repeatedly disrupting discussions, including bulk automated
  issues or comments.

## Moderation and reporting

Maintainers may ask someone to change their behavior, remove comments, close or
lock discussions, or restrict participation when this policy is violated.
Actions depend on the severity of the behavior and any repeated violations.

To report abusive content, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the issue
or comment. See [GitHub's reporting instructions](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
for the available reporting options. Avoid posting sensitive details in a
public issue.
